# Customer Churn Analysis – Power BI Dashboard



## Project Overview



This project focuses on analyzing customer churn using Microsoft Power BI. The dashboard explores customer attrition, customer segmentation, revenue trends, and retention patterns to understand factors that may contribute to customer churn.



The objective is to transform customer data into meaningful insights that help businesses understand churn behavior, identify customer segments, monitor revenue performance, and develop strategies to improve customer retention.



## Objectives



- Analyze customer churn patterns.

- Understand customer retention and attrition.

- Identify customer segments with different churn behaviors.

- Analyze revenue-related trends and customer value.

- Explore factors associated with customer churn.

- Support data-driven customer retention strategies.



## Tools Used



- Microsoft Power BI

- Power Query

- DAX

- Data Visualization



## Dashboard Pages



### 1. Executive Overview

- Overview of key customer and churn metrics.

- Summary of overall customer performance.

- High-level view of customer churn patterns.



### 2. Churn Analysis

- Analysis of customer churn.

- Comparison of churned and retained customers.

- Exploration of factors associated with customer attrition.



### 3. Customer Segmentation

- Analysis of customer groups and segments.

- Comparison of customer behavior across different segments.

- Identification of customer groups that may require retention strategies.



### 4. Revenue and Retention Analysis

- Analysis of revenue-related trends.

- Exploration of customer retention patterns.

- Understanding the relationship between customer retention and revenue.



## Key Insights



- **Customer Churn:** Analyzed customer churn patterns to understand customer attrition and retention behavior.

- **Customer Segmentation:** Explored customer segments to identify differences in customer behavior and churn patterns.

- **Revenue Analysis:** Examined revenue trends to understand the business impact of customer retention and attrition.

- **Retention Patterns:** Analyzed customer retention to understand the importance of maintaining long-term customer relationships.

- **Churn Factors:** Explored customer-related factors and patterns that may be associated with churn.



## Business Recommendations



- **Improve Customer Retention:** Develop targeted retention strategies for customers who show patterns associated with higher churn.

- **Customer Segmentation:** Create segment-specific strategies based on customer behavior, needs, and value.

- **Proactive Customer Engagement:** Identify customers who may be at risk of leaving and engage them through personalized communication and support.

- **Revenue Protection:** Prioritize retention strategies for valuable customer segments to help reduce potential revenue loss.

- **Enhance Customer Experience:** Identify areas where customer service, product experience, or engagement can be improved.

- **Continuous Churn Monitoring:** Regularly monitor churn and retention metrics to evaluate the effectiveness of retention strategies.
